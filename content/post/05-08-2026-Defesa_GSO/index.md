# August 2026 - Master Thesis Defense  - Mrs Gabriely Santos de Oliveira

Mrs. Gabriely successfully defended her master thesis, entitled “Montagem em escala cromossômica e anotação do genoma poliplóide da cana-de-açúcar SP80-3280”, on August 5th, 2026. The members of her evaluation committee were Prof. Claudia Barros Monteiro Vitorello (ESALQ/USP), Dr. Carolinne Rosa de Carvalho (UNICAMP), and her thesis supervisor Prof. Diego Mauricio Riano Pachon (CENA/USP).
