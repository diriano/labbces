# May 2026 - Master Thesis Defense  - Mrs Maria Camila Diaz Rodriguez

Mrs. Maria Camila successfully defended her master thesis, entitled “[Análise de elementos transponíveis em cana-de-açúcar, explorando genomas em escala cromossômica](https://doi.org/10.11606/D.64.2026.tde-20082026-105143)”, on May 29th, 2026. The members of her evaluation committee were Prof. Nathalia de Setta Costa (CCNG/UFABC), and Prof. Claudia Barros Monteiro Vitorello (ESALQ/USP), and her thesis supervisor Prof. Diego Mauricio Riano Pachon (CENA/USP). Mrs. Maria Camilia conuties with a PhD position at ESALQ/USP, we wish her the best!
